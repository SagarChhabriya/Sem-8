## Question 1

Company A signs an SLA with a cloud provider guaranteeing **99.9% availability** for a **28-day service period**. The service operates **10 hours per day** and costs **$40 per day**.

During the period, outages occurred with durations:

* 45 minutes
* 1 hour 15 minutes
* 30 minutes
* 2 hours

Service credit policy:

| Monthly Uptime | Credit |
| -------------- | ------ |
| < 99.9%        | 10%    |
| < 99%          | 20%    |

Tasks:

1. Calculate total downtime.
2. Calculate actual availability.
3. Check SLA violation.
4. Determine service credit.
5. Compute final payable amount.
